
# Data Sources

## 1. Historical Stock Prices
Candidate source: Yahoo Finance

Website: https://finance.yahoo.com/

Purpose:
- Historical stock prices
- Open, High, Low, Close, Volume

Collection method:
- Evaluate yfinance availability and terms of use.

## 2. Stock Metadata
Candidate source: Indonesia Stock Exchange (IDX)

Website: https://www.idx.co.id/

Purpose:
- Stock codes
- Company names
- Sector classifications, where available

Collection method:
- Inspect public pages and permitted data access methods.
- Verify website terms and data licensing before collection.

## 3. Data Quality Notes
- Record collection dates.
- Record missing or unavailable tickers.
- Document data limitations.
- Do not redistribute data without appropriate permission.
